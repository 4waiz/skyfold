# SKYFOLD: Clean Energy Challenge

A project by **Awaiz Ahmed**, built as a showcase for **ADIPEC 2026**. A VR and laptop experience for high-school students and recent graduates: stand at a lab table above a miniature coastal city and take the island to **net zero** in three quick sustainability puzzles. About three minutes from start to finish.

> An independent project inspired by ADNOC's public sustainability goals. It is not an official ADNOC product. The real-world facts shown in the game come from ADNOC announcements (2021–2026); see [docs/CLEAN_ENERGY.md](docs/CLEAN_ENERGY.md).

## The three puzzles

1. **Power the city 24/7.** Drop four of five energy blocks (solar, solar, nuclear, battery, gas) into the grid so the city has enough power by day *and* by night with zero emissions. Gas keeps the lights on but gets called out for its CO₂. Fact card: clean grid power from nuclear and solar.
2. **Capture the carbon.** Turn pipe tiles so CO₂ flows from a capture unit into an underground storage well. Connected pipes glow. Fact card: Al Reyadah, 800,000 tonnes of CO₂ a year.
3. **Blue carbon coast.** A 25-second round: plant mangroves on the mudflat spots as they glow. Fact card: 10 million mangroves by 2030, planted with drones.

Then the finale shows your score (up to 3000) and a rank: *Net Zero Hero*, *Clean Energy Innovator* or *Sustainability Cadet*. Every puzzle steps in and shows the answer if a player is stuck, so every visitor finishes in about three minutes. There is no way to fail.

## Play

Install-free: unzip the release and use one of:

- **Desktop shortcuts** (made by `tools\make_shortcuts.ps1`, or by `tools\package.ps1`): **SKYFOLD** (laptop) and **SKYFOLD - Quest 3** (VR via Link). They open the new build.
- In the game folder: `Play on this laptop.cmd`, `Play in VR (Quest 3 Link).cmd`, `Play Clean Energy Challenge (3 min).cmd`.
- Exhibition: `F5` restarts the challenge for the next visitor; results return to the title after 90 s by themselves.

Controls: [docs/CONTROLS.md](docs/CONTROLS.md). Quest setup: [docs/QUEST_LINK_SETUP.md](docs/QUEST_LINK_SETUP.md).

## Screenshots

Unity has to run to take real screenshots, so the build makes them for you. After building, run:

```
powershell -File tools\qa\run_qa.ps1 energy energyvr
```

This plays the whole challenge (laptop view, then the simulated headset view) and saves a screenshot at every step in `qa\_captures\energy\` and `qa\_captures\energyvr\`. In the game, F12 saves a screenshot at any time.

## What's under the hood

The lab runs on the original SKYFOLD engine: the Atelier terrace and plinth, the desktop/VR rigs with comfort options, the XR grab and point interactions, Noor's captions and the NURI companion, menus, HUD and the scene built from code. The lab boards themselves are built at runtime from simple shapes and the game's palette materials (`Assets/Skyfold/Scripts/Runtime/Energy`). The original episodes (*The Broken Causeway*, *The Courier Run*, *The Last Flight*) are still in the code for the QA scenarios.

## Build from source

Unity **6000.6.2f1** (URP 17.6, Input System, OpenXR 1.18, XR Interaction Toolkit 3.6). The scene, materials, input and XR settings are generated by editor code, so one command rebuilds everything:

```
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\build.ps1 -QA energy energyvr -Package
```

Automated QA scenarios: `tools\qa\run_qa.ps1 <scenario...>` (see [docs/QA_REPORT.md](docs/QA_REPORT.md)).
