# SKYFOLD: The Clean Flight — ADIPEC presentation script

About 3 minutes, alongside the live demo. Presented by **Awaiz Ahmed and Huda Mueen / Team Kanban**.
Audience: high school students and recent graduates. Keep it friendly and fast, and let them hold the controller as early as you can.

ADIPEC 2026 theme: *accelerating decarbonisation, energy security, resilience and industrial innovation across the global energy value chain.* The game touches all four:

| Theme | Where it shows up in the game |
| --- | --- |
| Decarbonisation | Wind and solar power the city; an electric courier car; a hydrogen-electric plane with no exhaust; a carbon-capture unit on the harbour |
| Energy security | The causeway carries the power cable to the island: rebuild it, and the island gets clean power again |
| Resilience | A storm is the only way to fail. An island microgrid with grid batteries keeps the airfield running |
| Industrial innovation | A green-hydrogen works makes the fuel cell you deliver, splitting water with renewable electricity |

## Before you start (2 minutes of setup)

- Laptop on mains power, the game closed, Desktop visible.
- **VR demo:** Meta Horizon Link running, headset on Link home, then the **SKYFOLD - Quest 3** shortcut. **Laptop demo:** the **SKYFOLD** shortcut, then *Three-minute demo*.
- Spectators watch the laptop: in VR it mirrors the headset view and adds a laptop-only overlay (objective, subtitles, storm timer, clean-grid meter).
- Between visitors: Pause → *Operator: reset demo* (or F5). The demo never touches campaign progress.

## Script

**0:00 — Hook (title screen)**
"This is SKYFOLD. A storm is heading for a little coastal city called Nacre Bay, and it has cut the city's clean power link. Your job: hold the whole city in your hands and fix it."

**0:15 — The miniature**
"This model is the real city, at 1:160. See the wind turbines and the solar farms? Grab the bridge piece, turn it and drop it into a glowing socket. The bridge carries the power cable, so fixing it reconnects the island."
*(Place the bridge on the east crossing. Point at the top-right meter.)*
"Watch the clean grid meter: it just jumped from 30 to 60 percent renewable."

**0:45 — The fold**
"Now fold down." *(X in VR, Q on the laptop.)* "Same city, real size. The bridge you just placed is a real bridge now."

**1:00 — The hydrogen run**
"The airfield's microgrid is dark. Grab the green-hydrogen fuel cell from the dock. It was made next door by splitting water with solar and wind power, and the only by-product is oxygen. Load it into the electric courier car and drive it across your bridge."

**1:45 — Microgrid and runway**
"Plug the cell into the microgrid. The grid batteries charge, the island lights come on, and the meter climbs again. Flip the solar beacon switch, then clear the flight."

**2:20 — Payoff**
"And there she goes: a hydrogen-electric plane. Hydrogen in, only water out." *(Takeoff, results screen.)* "The results show how clean you made the grid, plus a real energy fact."

**2:45 — Close**
"Everything in SKYFOLD, from the turbines to the plane, was built by code for this project. It runs on a laptop or a Quest 3. Want to try the full campaign? It's three episodes."

## Quick answers to likely questions

- *Is the clean-grid percentage real?* No. It is a game value that grows with each repair. The energy facts on the results screen are real, simplified explanations.
- *What is green hydrogen?* Hydrogen made by splitting water with renewable electricity, so making it gives off no CO2.
- *What does the carbon-capture unit do?* Its fans pull air through filters that trap CO2, which can then be stored underground.
- *Why a microgrid?* A small local grid with its own batteries keeps important places, like an airport, running when the main grid goes down.
- *Is VR finished?* The VR controls are in and work through Link. We are still testing on the headset.
- *Who made it?* Awaiz Ahmed and Huda Mueen (Team Kanban). The 3D models, music and most sounds come from scripts we wrote for this project. Noor's voice is local text-to-speech (Kokoro, stock voice), and the AI images are listed in the asset manifest.
