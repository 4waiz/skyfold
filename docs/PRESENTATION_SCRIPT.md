# SKYFOLD: The Last Flight — presentation script (hobby project)

About 3 minutes, alongside the live demo. Present it as a **hobby project by Awaiz Ahmed / Team Kanban**. It is not a SHIFT Game Jam entry and was made before any jam theme reveal, so do not pitch it as a jam submission.

## Before you start (2 minutes of setup)

- Laptop on mains power, the game closed, Desktop visible.
- **VR demo:** Meta Horizon Link running, headset on Link home, then the **SKYFOLD - Quest 3** shortcut. **Laptop-only demo:** the **SKYFOLD** shortcut, then *Three-minute demo*.
- Spectators watch the laptop: in VR it mirrors the left eye and adds a laptop-only overlay (objective, subtitles, storm timer).
- Between visitors: Pause → *Operator: reset demo* (or F5). The demo never touches campaign progress.

## Script

**0:00 — Hook (while the title screen orbits the plinth)**
"This is SKYFOLD. A storm is coming to a little coastal city called Nacre Bay, and it has broken the roads. You fix them by holding the whole city in your hands."

**0:15 — The miniature**
"This model on the plinth is the real city below us, at 1:160. Grab the bridge piece, turn it, and set it into a glowing socket. If it doesn't fit, it tells you why in plain words and gives the piece back."
*(Place the bridge on the east crossing.)*

**0:40 — The fold**
"Now the important part: fold down." *(X in VR, Q on the laptop.)* "Same city, real scale. The bridge you just placed is now a real bridge you can drive over. One city state drives both sizes, so whatever you change in your hands changes the street."

**1:00 — Carry and drive**
"The airfield needs power. Pick up the cell, load it into the courier car, climb in." *(Drive across the bridge.)* "In VR you can sit in the car with a stable horizon, or steer it from a fixed overlook if driving makes you queasy."

**1:45 — Power and beacons**
"Plug the cell into the substation, switch on the runway beacons, and clear the last flight."
*(NURI, the mechanical falcon, points the way. Noor talks you through it on the radio.)*

**2:20 — Payoff**
"And there it goes." *(Takeoff.)* "The storm timer is the only way to fail, and a retry puts you back at the last checkpoint. Medals come from how you actually played: time left, careful placements, hints."

**2:45 — Close**
"SKYFOLD runs on this laptop with keyboard and mouse, or on a Quest 3 through Link, from one install-free folder. All the models, music and most sounds were made by scripts for this project. Noor's voice is local text-to-speech. Thanks for playing."

## Honest answers to likely questions

- *Is VR finished?* The VR build path and controls are implemented. Headset testing is still to do, and the QA report says so.
- *Is this a jam game?* No. It's a hobby project made before any jam theme was announced.
- *Who made the art?* Everything was procedurally generated by Blender Python scripts written for this project. Noor's voice is AI text-to-speech (Kokoro, stock voice). Any AI images are listed in the asset manifest. None are in this build.
