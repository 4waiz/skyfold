## Voiceover (Noor) - Audio/VO/

Generated by `tools/audio/make_vo.py build --voice af_heart --speed 0.8`, which runs the kanban-motion skill's `scripts/voiceover.py` (kokoro-onnx 0.6.1, fp32 ONNX export of Kokoro-82M) once for all lines and splits the result at its exact line bounds. **VO chain**: 24 -> 48 kHz polyphase resample, 60 Hz high-pass, trim to ~40 ms lead / ~150 ms tail, 5 ms fades, loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS with a look-ahead true-peak limiter (<= -1.5 dBTP; it only catches sub-millisecond plosive spikes), mono 16-bit with TPDF dither. Voice chosen by objective audition (reports/vo_audition.json); every line transcribed with faster-whisper small.en for WER and letter-name checks (reports/vo_qa.json). The 'Script' column is the exact TTS input (`spoken` if present, else the caption).
