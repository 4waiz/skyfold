# Art manifest - characters, vehicles and the power cell

All entries are original procedural models built by Blender 5.2 Python scripts in `tools/blender/`. Rebuild everything (models, previews, verification, contact sheet) with `blender -b --factory-startup -P tools/blender/chars_build_all.py` (add `-- --no-render` to skip renders); every script also runs on its own. No external assets, textures, scans or generative-AI output were used. The scripts were written in an AI-assisted coding session (Claude Code); the geometry itself comes only from those scripts. Materials use the shared `SKY_*` palette names from `docs/ART_SPEC.md`. Every mesh has the vertex colour attribute `Col` holding a soft AO bake (white = open).

Axes: Unity forward (+Z) = Blender -Y and Unity +X = Blender -X, so `x_u = -x_b, y_u = z_b, z_u = -y_b`. Assets face Unity +Z, and an asset's left side is Unity -X (Blender +X). All pivot positions, empty positions and bounds are listed in Unity axes (metres) in `source-art/models_manifest_chars.json`, which `tools/blender/chars_verify.py` writes from the exported FBX files.

## Hierarchy and pivot notes

- **nuri**: `Nuri` (body centre) > `Body` > `Head` (neck joint) > `Lens` (lens centre), `Beak` (beak root); `Body` > `Wing_L`, `Wing_R` (shoulder joints: rotate about local Z to flap), `Tail` (tail base), `Thruster` (emissive disc centre). Wings are modelled half-spread. Span 0.56 m, beak-to-tail 0.38 m. Dark hood, brow and malar wedge in SKY_Ink; the lens is two glowing rings around a dark pupil in a brass bezel. The detail pass added SKY_Teal, SKY_Coral and SKY_Sandstone accents.
- **glove_L / glove_R**: root = controller grip point (handle axis along Unity Y). `Open` and `Grip` share that pivot; show one at a time. Length 0.185 m. `ANCHOR_wristui` (glove_L) sits at the bracer face centre: local -Z points out of the face (Unity -X), +X toward the fingertips, +Y toward the little finger. The Euler angles are (0, 90, 180).
- **courier_car**: root = ground, centred between the axles (wheelbase 2.2 m, track 1.33 m). `Wheel_*` pivots are at the hub centres (r 0.32 m) and spin about local X. `SteeringWheel` (child of `Body`) sits at the hub with local rotation (35, 0, 0): its local +Z runs down the steering column, so spin it about local Z on top of its rest rotation. `ANCHOR_driver_eye` is at 1.15 m. `ANCHOR_door` is on the left sill. `ANCHOR_exit_left/right` are at ±1.4 m, ground level. `SOCKET_cargo` is the bottom centre of the upright cell in the rear cradle (0.875 m up).
- **convoy_shuttle / city_car_a / city_car_b**: root = ground, centred between the axles; `Body` + `Wheel_FL/FR/RL/RR` at the hub centres. The shuttle's passenger door is on its right flank.
- **aircraft**: root = ground under the main-gear centre. `Body` > `Door` (left side, pivot on its bottom hinge edge; open it by rotating about local Z, about +125 deg swings the top outward and down into a stair), `Prop_L` / `Prop_R` (hub centres; spin about local Z), `LIGHT_nav_L` (left tip, red-ish lens), `LIGHT_nav_R` (right tip, green-ish lens), `LIGHT_beacon`, `LIGHT_landing`. `ANCHOR_door` (child of the root) is at ground level outside the door. Gold is used only as the thin pinstripe, the fin band and the spinner tips, as the brief asked.
- **power_cell**: root mesh `PowerCell` (bottom centre) > `Core` (core centre, separate emissive mesh), `ANCHOR_handle` (suggested hand attach point). 0.35 m tall, 0.14 m diameter.

## Export note (affects every nested FBX)

ART_SPEC's FBX call uses `bake_space_transform=True`. In Blender 5.2 that exporter path writes wrong local matrices for objects nested two or more levels below the root: they get a stray 90 deg X rotation and an unconverted offset. `chars_common._patch_fbx_exporter()` corrects `ObjectWrapper.fbx_object_matrix` for mesh and empty chains (local = G * local * G^-1) at export time. The operator call is otherwise exactly ART_SPEC's. `chars_verify.py` checks the raw FBX transforms and a Blender re-import against the build intent. All 12 files pass: names, parenting, pivots, SKY_* materials only, `Col` on every mesh, no n-gons, and tri budgets.
