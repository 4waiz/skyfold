- Tone preset: cinematic (the same direction as the landscape cut, tighter)
- Hook: THIS CITY / FITS IN / YOUR HANDS over the tilt-shift canal glide, with Noor on the radio: "A storm is coming… and it has broken our roads."

- Layout: key text stays out of the top 15% and bottom 20% (YouTube Shorts UI). Objective cards sit at y≈330 px; radio badges, chips and the location card sit above the bottom safe zone.
