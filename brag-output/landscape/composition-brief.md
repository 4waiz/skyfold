## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: a warm-coastal disaster-movie trailer for a tiny city
- Interpretation: slow confident camera moves, hard cuts on impacts, big letter-spaced uppercase statements on the dramatic beats, and a game-HUD objective tracker for the gameplay beats. Restraint in Act I, escalation through the storm, release at the takeoff.
- Angle: one city, two scales. The miniature in your hands and the real street are the same place, and the edit makes the viewer feel the fold.
- Hook: out of black, Noor on the radio ("This is Noor. A storm is coming… and it has broken our roads.") over a tilt-shift glide across what looks like a real canal city.
- Outro / punchline: SKYFOLD folds in letter by letter, then THE LAST FLIGHT, the two platforms, then MADE BY AWAIZ AHMED.
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, abstract filler, any claim the project does not make, and any mention of a game jam.

## Audio
- Audio role: cinematic support with narration
- Audio arc: radio static and wonder, then a hush before the bridge rises and an impact as it locks; momentum through the drive, dread at the storm, full release at the takeoff, and the finale ringing under the credit
- Music: the game's own score, built into one bed: `music_menu_loop` → `music_drive_loop` → `music_storm_loop` → `music_finale` (from 12 s in)
- Music treatment: ducked to 0.3 under every voice line; hushed to 0.12 just before the bridge rises; fades out with the last frame
- Music cue guidance: `hyperframes beats` found no beats in the ducked ambient bed; the grid comes from the generator (finale 96 BPM, beat 0 at file start). The finale's key change sits on the lift-off at about 72.3 s. The title letters start on the beat at 79.8 s, and the subtitle lands on the beat at 81.05 s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. The music bed's low end swells the warm glow behind the title; its top end lights the gold rule. Data is pre-extracted per frame and sampled with `tl.call`.
- Audio-coupled moments: caption slams (soft impacts), reject / rotate / snap, bridge assemble, fold whoosh, cell insert, car roll and motor, substation hum, thunder on the lightning, beacon ticks rising in pitch with the sweep, door chime, lever clunk, spool-up / takeoff / fly-by, card slides on the paper-fold letters
- SFX selection guidance: the game's own effects for every physical action; the brag library's warm low-risk impacts (`impactSoft_heavy_*`) and card sounds (`casino/card-slide-*`, `card-place-*`) for graphics
- Exact SFX choice: in `tools/trailer/edits.py`
- Audio files: `composition/assets/audio/music.wav` (bed + ambience, ducked), `vo.wav`, `sfx.wav`, levelled together to about -14 LUFS
